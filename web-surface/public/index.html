# gunnchOS3k MLV Hackathons **Public landing page and knowledge base** for planned **Summer 2026** community hack days in Northwest Indiana — starting with the **Merrillville pilot Hack Day**. > **You do not need a thousand-dollar laptop to participate.** If you have a phone, curiosity, and willingness to learn, you can join, build, have fun, and compete. ---

Mission

**gunnchOS3k MLV** is a Northwest Indiana-rooted technology education and innovation organization. We make computing, AI, games, and emerging technology accessible through **beginner-friendly, phone-first** hackathons where students, professionals, and curious community members can learn, collaborate, and build projects they are proud to show. This repository hosts: 1. A **static website** in [`/docs`](docs/index.htm
